Our Services

What We Do

01. Import & Export Services

We facilitate international trade transactions for businesses seeking to import goods into Lebanon or export products to international markets.

For businesses without their own import/export infrastructure, we offer coordinated trading arrangements through our company, subject to applicable legal, customs and commercial requirements.

We manage coordination between customers, overseas suppliers, shipping agents and relevant service providers to simplify the entire process.

Our services include:

  • Import Transaction Coordination
  • Export Transaction Coordination
  • Supplier & Customer Coordination
  • Trade Documentation
  • Shipping Arrangements
  • Import/Export Facilitation

02. General Trading & Wholesale

In addition to facilitating transactions for customers, Hbayter SBH Group conducts general trading activities on its own account.

We source products from international manufacturers and suppliers, arrange procurement and transportation, and trade goods for resale in the Lebanese and international markets.

Our services include:

  • International Product Sourcing
  • Supplier Identification
  • Product Procurement
  • Wholesale Trading
  • Import & Resale
  • Distribution

03. Logistics & Cargo Delivery

We coordinate cargo transportation, collection and delivery to support our international trading activities and customer requirements.

From shipment coordination to final delivery, we aim to provide practical and reliable logistics solutions.

Our services include:

  • Cargo Collection
  • Shipment Coordination
  • Local Transportation
  • Door-to-Door Delivery
